                  How the Market Barometer Works

                  Scoring System

                  RISK SCORE -5 to +3

                  Measures market fear and financial stress:

                  • VXX (Volatility): >55 = Panic (-2/-1), 40-55 = Elevated (-1/0), <30 = Calm (+1)
                  • HYG/LQD (Credit): <0.85 = Stress (-2/-1), >0.90 = Healthy (+1)
                  • SPY/TLT (Stocks vs Bonds): 1W >+2% = Risk-On (+1), 1W <-2% = Risk-Off (-1)

                  ↑ Arrow = worsening, ↓ Arrow = improving

                  CYCLE SCORE -2 to +2

                  Measures economic expansion or contraction:

                  • XLY/XLP (Consumer): 1W >+1% = Expansion (+1), 1W <-1% = Contraction (-1)
                  • CPER/GLD (Copper/Gold): 1W >+2% = Reflation (+1), 1W <-2% = Deflation (-1)

                  BREADTH -1 to +1

                  Market participation width - KEY regime modifier:

                  • ≥7/11 positive (Strong): Score +1 - Broad participation, never gives RISK-OFF
                  • 5-6/11 positive (Mixed): Score 0 - Moderate participation, Risk/Cycle decides
                  • ≤4/11 positive (Weak): Score -1 - Narrow leadership, never gives RISK-ON

                  Breadth has VETO power: Weak breadth blocks RISK-ON, Strong breadth blocks RISK-OFF.

                  Regime Logic

                  RISK-ON

                  Risk/Cycle positive or neutral + Breadth mixed/strong (≥5/11). Favor equities and cyclicals.

                  RISK-OFF

                  Risk/Cycle negative + Breadth weak/mixed (≤6/11). Favor bonds and safe havens.

                  ROTATION

                  Risk negative + Cycle positive + Breadth mixed/strong (≥5/11). Money rotating, not fleeing.

                  CAUTION

                  Conflicting signals: Either positive signals with weak breadth, OR negative risk with strong breadth.

                  NEUTRAL

                  Mixed or all-neutral signals. No clear direction. Balanced allocation recommended.
